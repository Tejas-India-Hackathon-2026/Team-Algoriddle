# Bihar Yatra 🗺️

AI-Powered Smart & Safe Tourism Platform for Bihar

Bihar Yatra is a smart tourism platform designed to help travelers discover, plan, and experience Bihar through personalized travel planning, intelligent route recommendations, destination discovery, safety assistance, and a digital tourism passport.

---

🌟 Overview

Bihar is home to some of India's most important historical, spiritual, cultural, and natural destinations. However, travelers often face difficulties in discovering destinations, planning personalized trips, finding suitable routes, and accessing reliable travel information.

**Bihar Yatra** aims to solve these problems by bringing multiple tourism services into one intelligent platform.

The platform helps users:

- Discover tourist destinations across Bihar
- Create personalized travel plans
- Find optimized and safer routes
- Explore local culture and cuisine
- Access emergency assistance
- Track their travel journey
- Collect digital destination stamps

---
 🎯 Problem Statement

Tourists visiting Bihar may face several challenges:

- Lack of personalized travel planning
- Difficulty discovering lesser-known destinations
- Traditional navigation focuses mainly on distance or travel time
- Safety concerns during last-mile travel
- Limited connectivity in remote tourism areas
- Difficulty finding reliable local services
- Tourism information is scattered across different platforms

---

💡 Our Solution

Bihar Yatra provides a unified tourism experience where users can:

**Discover → Plan → Navigate → Explore → Stay Safe → Collect Memories**

The platform combines tourism discovery, intelligent trip planning, route optimization, safety features, and gamification into a single application.

---

 🚀 Key Features

## 🧳 1. AI Trip Planner

Users can create personalized trips based on:

- Starting location
- Destination
- Travel dates
- Duration
- Number of travelers
- Budget
- Travel style
- Transport preference
- Interests
- Special requirements

The system generates a customized itinerary according to the user's preferences.

---

## 🗺️ 2. Smart Route Finder

The application provides different route preferences such as:

- Shortest Route
- Fastest Route
- Safest Route
- Heritage Route
- Scenic Route

The Safe Route system can consider factors beyond distance, such as safety conditions and important nearby facilities.
